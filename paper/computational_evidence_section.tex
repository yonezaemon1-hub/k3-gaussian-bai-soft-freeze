\section{Computational evidence}\label{sec:computational-evidence}

The main computational certificate is exact rather than empirical.  The supplied SymPy audit verifies the rational identities used in the proof, the static-oracle polynomial identity, the discriminant calculations, the quintic identity, and strict Bernstein-coefficient positivity on five rational subintervals.  A successful run reports \texttt{PASS\_EXACT\_SYMBOLIC\_AUDIT} and \texttt{BERNSTEIN\_INTERVALS\_PASS = 5/5}.

For presentation, \texttt{scripts/export\_certified\_rate\_curve.py} samples only the already-proved lower envelope $\min\{E_A(r),E_B\}$ using exact rational arithmetic.  Representative values are:

The computational evidence therefore has two roles: exact symbolic certification of the proof algebra and a reproducible visualization of the certified rate geometry.  It does not determine the exact value of $P_3$ or establish optimality of the proof-friendly pilot fraction $14/25$.
