# Computational tables

- `certified_rate_table.csv` — selected exact-rational samples of the proved lower envelope `min(E_A(r), E_B)`.
- `EXACT_SYMBOLIC_AUDIT.txt` — frozen exact symbolic/Bernstein proof audit already in the repository.
- `scripts/export_certified_rate_curve.py` — exporter for a denser CSV curve when needed.

The symbolic audit is the primary computational certificate. The CSV table is a visualization of proved formulas, not a stochastic experiment.
